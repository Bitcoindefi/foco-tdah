Hi, I'm Leonardo, founder of EnFoco.

EnFoco is a focus app for adults with ADHD. It does one thing: one task, one light on.

You pick one task. You take a small first step. You turn the light on, and you keep it on while you work.

No streaks. No guilt. Your data stays on your phone.

The problem

⏱ 25 sstarts at 0:15ends at 0:40

Open slide 2 →

People with ADHD know what they need to do. The hard part is to start, and to keep going.

Doctor Barkley says ADHD is a problem with starting, planning and memory. A deadline only feels real when it is very close.

A 2026 study, still a preprint, found this: when a plan fails, people feel shame. And they don't like apps that watch them.

Most apps give you long lists, streaks and tracking. For people with ADHD, that means a big red list, and more guilt.

How it works

⏱ 25 sstarts at 0:55ends at 1:20

Open slide 4 →

This is the loop. Type, Enter, Enter.

One: I write a task, like "Reply to the landlord". Or I pick a task from today's list.

Two: the app gives me a small first step. For example: write only the first sentence. I can change it. I pick 2, 10 or 25 minutes.

Three: I tap "Light up and start". The light turns on and a big timer starts.

Four: when I finish, the light goes to today in the calendar. The next task is one tap away.

No guilt by design

⏱ 15 sstarts at 1:40ends at 1:55

If I stop, nothing turns red.

Vision, limits and next steps

⏱ 40 sstarts at 3:30ends at 4:10

Open slide 10 →

Our goal: any adult with ADHD can start the next small task in less than one minute. No shame. No tracking. Their data stays private.

In Portugal, about 1.5 to 3 percent of adults have ADHD. Less than one in five get the right diagnosis and treatment. In the world, it's about 2.5 percent of adults.

To be honest, what it is not yet: we have not tested it with people with ADHD. It is not a treatment. And we have not tested it on cheap phones.

Next steps: first, two weeks to make it stable. Then a test with five to eight adults with ADHD, with Brainstorm. Then an app you can install, with private sync and Spanish.

We will measure two things: Start and Stay. These numbers are goals, not results.

One more idea, not built yet: a WhatsApp assistant with Wexio. You send a message or a voice note, and the task goes to your list and your calendar.

After the pitch

Likely questions

Has this been validated?

No. The design comes from public sources. Testing with adults with ADHD is our next step, which is why we want to work with Brainstorm.

Is it a medical tool?

No. It doesn't diagnose or treat, and it doesn't replace professional support.

Does the app watch the user?

No. It only knows whether the tab is visible and whether there was a touch. It doesn't record that, and session time counts anyway.

Where is the data?

Only in the browser, in localStorage. There are buttons to export and delete. The only network requests for the app itself are the 3D library and two fonts. The lofi music is generated on the device. The voice assistant uses this browser's built-in speech service; EnFoco does not send voice to its own servers.

Is there AI?

Not today in the live app. The first step comes from keyword rules, and the in-app voice assistant is a small set of commands on top of the browser's speech APIs. If AI is added, it will be opt-in, suggest the first step, and stay editable. A WhatsApp assistant powered by Wexio is a proposed next step, not built.

Will it run on a cheap phone?

It's built to be light: few polygons, one light per scene, about 30 fps, and a plain HTML view without WebGL. We haven't tested real low-end devices yet. That's step one on our list.

What did you cut?

Demo personas, motivation boosters, co-focus, energy and mood check-ins, and rhythm-of-the-day charts. They can come back only if testing asks for them.

How will you make money?

Freemium. The daily loop stays free. Premium features — sync, two-way calendar sync (Google, Apple/iCloud, Outlook, CalDAV/ICS), routines, an AI coach, insights — are proposed, and so is a B2B channel with associations, clinics, schools and employers. We are not stating a price today; that is a hypothesis to validate in product-market fit testing, which is why we are asking TESLA Venture Studio for their PMF program.

Why TESLA Venture Studio?

Because the next risk is not "can we build the loop?" — we already have a working prototype. The risk is market: will people with ADHD use it, what should stay free, and how we reach them in Portugal and then the EU. That is exactly the Market Development and Product-Market Fit Program.

And Wexio?

Wexio is a conversational automation platform for WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram and the web, with voice-note transcription. It is not a voice platform. The in-app voice assistant is already live. We are asking Wexio to power a proposed WhatsApp assistant, not yet built: you send a text or a voice note, it goes into the brain dump, and the task lands in whichever calendar you use — Google, Apple/iCloud, Outlook, or any CalDAV or ICS calendar, two-way — plus a daily nudge with today's recommended task, with no guilt. It would stay optional, and it would not replace the quiet, local loop we have today.
